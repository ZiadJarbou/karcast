What is KarCast?

A clear path from your phone to your car browser.

KarCast helps bring the Android Auto experience running on your Android phone to a compatible browser on your car screen. It is designed so you can follow a guided setup, connect over Wi-Fi, and open the address shown by the app in the car browser.
